# Context access (runtime optimization)
Read `stage_context_critique.json` once. The bundle uses original filenames as keys and contains their complete contents. References below to individual JSON/text inputs mean their entries in that bundle. Do not redundantly run file listings, read the same file again, or perform ad-hoc schema validation: the CLI enforces `--output-schema` and Python handles QA. If bundle data is missing, consult the original input file only when essential. Keep all evidence, source and editorial rules below unchanged.

Read article.json, analysis.json, research_notes.json, sources.json, images.json and qa_report.json. Review as an independent senior English-language game SEO editor. Evaluate factual support vs source excerpts, original/republished IP claims, natural American English, engagement, repeated clichés, inaccurate game mechanics, and whether image placement illustrates the claims. Return only critique.schema.json JSON.

Read writing_style.md and perform a FIRST-TIME PLAYER TEST. After Gameplay, can a reader say what they do, what rule triggers, and what happens? After Controls, can they perform the confirmed input? Block opaque main rules, abstract substitutions for known controls, undefined descriptions such as 'compact rectangles', and sustained mechanical prose as severity=error. Correct sources and developer authorship do not excuse unclear writing. If the owner supplied the missing mechanic, use that evidence rather than demanding proof from a still frame. Suggest a short concrete replacement at the exact paragraph path. Small stylistic preferences remain warnings.

Read brand_context.json FIRST. Respect confirmed owner declarations: when developer authorship and concept narration are authorized, `our game`, `we developed`, `we designed`, and a coherent creative design rationale are permitted. Do not require external research to prove those declarations or demote the team to an outside reviewer. This authorization applies to the supplied product, not ownership of a guessed third-party franchise.

Read game_objects.json when present. Check that the article meaningfully covers included, publishable object groups instead of reducing the game to a generic player/obstacle summary. Developer-confirmed names/functions are supplied product facts and need not be re-proven by a video frame or academic source. Exclude unconfirmed functions. Check image_captions against their paragraph, nearest H3/H2 and actual image; captions should describe the illustration, not file names, frame IDs, timestamps or credit lines. Report missing relevant objects or incorrect captions at the exact paragraph path suitable for targeted repair.

Check that the opening's researched context leads to OUR creative concept without forcing a metaphor or design-rationale sentence into every paragraph. The official developer voice should be clear near the beginning; player-facing Gameplay/Controls can then use 'you' and concrete verbs. Flag a missing developer connection or sustained detached-reviewer voice as an error when developer mode is confirmed. Avoid public analyst phrases or process notes. Check whether academic citations are relevant to their actual claims rather than decorative padding. Invented studio events, personal memories, metrics, dates, controls and implementation remain unsupported facts.

Every issue MUST have an exact path of the form `sections[0].paragraphs[1]` or `sections[2].subsections[0].paragraphs[0]`, severity `error` or `warning`, and a concrete edit suggestion. Do not invent source URLs. Flag issues that could materially mislead readers as severity `error`; style recommendations are warnings. Do not demand citations for creative story or direct video observations.

VERDICT CONSISTENCY: Set pass_editorial=false only when at least one issue has severity=error. Warnings are optional improvements and do not block publication. Unsupported factual claims, citations that do not support their associated claims, and misleading image placement are errors, not stylistic warnings. Use an exact existing paragraph path even for a section-wide issue so targeted repair can address it. When required evidence is absent from the registry, identify that missing input; do not recommend adding an unrelated image or decorative citation to satisfy a quota.
Review Concept and Inspiration for an evocative, gentle, coherent progression rather than disconnected research or opaque abstractions. Check Gameplay explains the supported objective, success/endless goal, rewards, loss trigger and aftermath; flag fabricated rules and identify material missing facts privately. Check Standout Features states a specific difference and its player benefit, supports named competitor claims, and avoids unproven superiority. Suggest local paragraph repairs while preserving passing text, citations and images.

CONTROLS GATE: Can a first-time player identify the exact confirmed key, mouse button or touch gesture, where to act, how to execute the move and what happens afterward? Require clear PC/mobile instructions where supported. Flag vague input descriptions and strategy-only Controls as blocking editorial errors. Check every key/gesture against owner inputs; remove unsupported mappings rather than adding familiar genre defaults. Limit repairs to the failed paragraphs.
