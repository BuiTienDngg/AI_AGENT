SECTION-SPECIFIC EDITORIAL DIRECTION:

Let each paragraph pick up the previous idea. Avoid disconnected research summaries, abstract design jargon, repeated claims of inspiration and invented studio memories. Without it, compare to a generic 2D presentation and keep the focus on our own confirmed design. Frame enjoyment as intended appeal, not measured superiority or a universal claim that our game is more fun.
